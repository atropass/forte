# Violation types

Codes follow the team catalog (`legal/violations_catalog.source.json`); X01–X02 are additional labelled
types not in the catalog. The API `type` is the catalog name; `type_code` and `subtype` are extra fields.
Timecode convention = the moment the triggering behaviour **starts** (the cashier stands up / turns away;
the visitor enters; money is handed over), as in the training labels.
