## A. Правила осуществления кассовых операций и операций по инкассации банкнот, монет и ценностей в БВУ ...
* Постановление Правления НБРК от 29 ноября 2019 года № 231; code V1900019680
* https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V1900019680
* п.5: the bank equips a кассовый узел; its layout is governed by the premises rules (doc B).
* п.9: the bank provides special equipment for checking banknote validity and authenticity.
* п.12: employees' personal belongings, cash and valuables are stored **outside** the кассовый узел; medicines and communication devices without photo/video are allowed (edition of 10.11.2025 No. 71).
* п.13: full individual liability agreements with cashiers / persons responsible for safekeeping of cash.
* п.14: the bank organises cash operations to exclude conflicts of interest and conditions for theft of cash and valuables.
* п.19 пп.1: when accepting cash, the cashier **visually identifies the client against an ID document** (or digital-documents service data). пп.2: per-note count with authenticity/validity check per пп.57–58.
* п.24: accepting cash from a collection unit: based on the accompanying document; the cashier checks the document details (пп.1), registers the amount (пп.2), checks bag integrity (пп.3) and sealing (пп.4). **No explicit "collector shows ID" wording in п.24.**
* п.41: counting/sorting equipment must check ≥2 machine-readable security features (Rules No. 230).
* п.57: validity checked per Rules No. 230 (Правила определения платежности банкнот и монет, постановление НБРК от 29.11.2017 № 230).
* пп.59–62: procedure for doubtful / suspected counterfeit notes (act in presence of client and head of cash desk, etc.).
* п.101: before departure, collectors receive a power of attorney (доверенность, приложение 4) and other documents → basis for "collector must present documents".

## B. Правила организации охраны и устройства помещений БВУ ...
* Постановление Правления НБРК от 24 февраля 2020 года № 14; registered with MoJ on 27.02.2020 under No. 20075; code V2000020075
* https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2000020075 (amended by No. 105 of 21.09.2020, No. 125 of 19.12.2022, No. 88 of 24.12.2024)
* п.7: the кассовый узел includes the vault/safe room, pre-vault, cash desk and counting room.
* п.16 пп.1: each cash desk has a separate entrance **with a door that locks**.
* п.19 пп.1: the counting room has a separate entrance with a door that locks.
